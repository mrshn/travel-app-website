This is the page built in the chat before the app existed: first as a single interactive plan, then redrawn with original illustrations and day route maps. It's kept here as it was on 29 September 2026. The app now carries the same plan and adds the live guide, your progress and your notes.

What's on it:

- **Plan**: the five days as a timeline, with the options you can pick (ride from the airport, Friday and Saturday nights, the Sunday noon) and the Colosseum Saturday/Sunday swap.
- **Book**: what to book and when, with official links.
- **Places**: sights, food and photo spots with open days for 9–12 October and honest verdicts on viral places.
- **Nights**: how a Roman night works, crawls, clubs by night and how to get home.
- **Info**: documents, money with a euro-to-lira converter, phone, packing, getting around, safety, departure day, budget and phrases.

Open it with the button above. It works on its own, but it doesn't save ticks or notes (the app does).
