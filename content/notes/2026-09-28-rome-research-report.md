# Conquer Rome: Your Complete Four-Day Plan

**Your personal plan · Thu 8 – Mon 12 October 2026 · Solo, first trip abroad · Base: YellowSquare Rome**

## 1. Your trip at a glance

- You land late on **Thu 8 Oct**, get **three full days (Fri 9, Sat 10, Sun 11)** plus the **morning of Mon 12 Oct**, and fly home at **14:50 on Mon 12 Oct**.
- The three hard bookings sit on the right days: **Vatican Museums Fri 9 Oct 08:00** (closed Sun 11), **Colosseum Sat 10 Oct 08:30**, **Borghese Gallery Sun 11 Oct 15:00** (closed Mon 12).
- Your base is **YellowSquare**, an award-winning party hostel (Best Hostel in Italy, 2026 HOSCARs; 250+ guests), so people to go out with are never far away.
- **Sun 11 and Mon 12 are the tricky days:** many trattorias close on Sunday; on Monday the Borghese, Castel Sant'Angelo and Largo Argentina are shut and Trevi has a maintenance day.
- Doing everything costs about **€150 a day (≈8,360 TL)** on top of the hostel; a saver version is about **€103 a day (≈5,740 TL)**.
- The three real risks are a **police document check when you land from Spain**, missing the **last train from the airport**, and **pickpockets and bar scams**. This guide shows you how to beat all three.

## 3. Before you go: documents, money, phone, apps, packing

### Documents and visa

**Read your visa sticker now (2 minutes):**

| Field | What you want to see |
|---|---|
| **VALID FOR** | "Schengen States" / "États Schengen" = valid in Italy. A list of countries instead (for example only "ES") is a limited visa, **not valid for Italy** unless "IT" is on it ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schengen_visa)) |
| **FROM … UNTIL** | "Until" must be **12 Oct 2026 or later** |
| **TYPE** | "C" = short stay |
| **ENTRIES** | 1, 2 or MULT. Barcelona→Rome is inside Schengen, so it **doesn't use an entry** |
| **DURATION OF VISIT … DAYS** | Count all your days in Schengen on this trip (arrival and departure days included) up to 12 Oct; they must fit ([EU calculator](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/border-crossing/short-stay-calculator_en)) |

*(Based on the standard sticker layout; the official annex text couldn't be checked.)*

- **No entry stamp? Normal.** The EU Entry/Exit System (EES) replaced stamps on 10 Apr 2026; your face and fingerprints were recorded at entry ([European Commission](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/smart-borders/entry-exit-system_en)).
- **Carry your original passport at all times. It's the law.** A foreigner who can't show a passport and proof of legal stay when police ask, without good reason, faces **arrest of up to 1 year and a fine of up to €2,000** (D.Lgs. 286/1998, art. 6(3)) ([Brocardi](https://www.brocardi.it/testo-unico-immigrazione/titolo-ii/capo-i/art6.html)). Keep it in a zipped inner pocket.
- **Your hostel handles the "dichiarazione di presenza".** Non-EU visitors entering Italy from another Schengen country must declare their presence within 8 days. For stays under 90 days in a hotel, the form you sign at check-in counts, and the property informs the police ([Università di Bologna](https://www.unibo.it/it/studiare/iscrizioni-tasse-e-altre-procedure/lauree-e-lauree-magistrali/regole-ingresso-soggiorno-in-italia/all-arrivo-in-italia-dichiarazione-di-presenza-e-permesso-di-soggiorno-per-studenti-di-scambio); [InfoImmigrazione](https://www.infoimmigrazione.com/dichiarazione-di-presenza-stranieri/)). A licensed hostel registers guests the same way. Show your passport and sign.
- **Police-check folder** (paper + phone): passport with visa, hostel booking, Pegasus e-ticket for 12 Oct, travel insurance, cards plus some cash as proof of money. Italy's checks on arrivals from Spain look at identity, passport, visa and your right to move in Schengen; people without documents are the ones refused ([AlphabetCity](https://alphabetcity.it/2026/09/01/schengen-italia-e-spagna-prorogano-i-controlli-verifiche-in-porti-e-aeroporti-fino-al-16-e-22-settembre-chi-rischia-lo-stop-alla-frontiera/)).
- **Insurance:** your visa insurance covers emergency care, hospital and repatriation ([European Commission](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen-borders-and-visa/visa-policy/applying-schengen-visa_en)). Save its 24/7 number.
- **Backups:** photos of your passport and visa in the cloud, plus one paper copy kept apart from the passport.

### Money

- **Bring a Visa or Mastercard, ideally two from different banks.** Rome's contactless transport accepts Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB, UnionPay, Maestro and V Pay. **TROY isn't on the list** ([ATAC](https://www.atac.roma.it/biglietti-e-abbonamenti/tap-and-go)), so a TROY-only card will probably fail on the metro, at Trevi's card-only gate and in many shops.
- Cards work almost everywhere: Italian shops can be fined for refusing card payment ([it.wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_of_sale)).
- **Always pay in EUR, never TRY.** If a machine offers "pay in TRY", refuse: that's dynamic currency conversion, with markups up to 18% ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_currency_conversion)).
- **Avoid Euronet and stand-alone "tourist" ATMs** (high fees, bad rates) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euronet_Worldwide)). Use an ATM at a real bank branch.
- **Bring €100–150 in cash** from home. Carry €50–100; lock the rest away.
- **Wise and Revolut aren't available to Türkiye residents** ([Wise TR](https://wise.com/tr/card/); [Revolut](https://help.revolut.com/en-US/help/profile-and-plan/profile-plan/verifying-identity/what-countries-are-supported/)). Ask your bank about its foreign-transaction fee.
- **Rate: €1 = 55.80 TL** (ECB, 25 Sep 2026) ([ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-try.en.html)). €10 ≈ 560 TL; €50 ≈ 2,790 TL; €120 ≈ 6,700 TL.

**Pork note:** of Rome's four famous pastas, **only cacio e pepe is pork-free**. ### Coffee, aperitivo and tipping

- **Stand at the bar:** tables cost **2–3× more**. Pay at the till, then give the receipt to the barista ([Food Republic](https://www.foodrepublic.com/1666978/sitting-vs-standing-rome-coffee-cafes/)). Espresso **€1.20–1.50**; latte **€2.50–3.50**, or **€7** at the Spanish Steps ([Rome Tourist](https://www.rometourist.net/rome-2026-price-guide-living-the-dolce-vita-without-losing-your-shirt-%F0%9F%87%AE%F0%9F%87%B9)).
- Cappuccino is a **morning** drink (custom, not a rule).
- **Aperitivo 19:00–21:00:** €5 spritz at Bar San Calisto or Necci; €12–16 on rooftops near Termini and Monti.
- **Tipping isn't expected**: round up for great table service.
- Don't eat while walking; refill your bottle at the free **nasoni** fountains, city water safe to drink ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasone)).

### Late-night food

- **Arrival night:** Mercato Centrale closes 23:30. **Lambiase** (Sallustiano) sells sorchetta pastries "all night except Mondays" ([Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/rome/guides/best-bakeries-rome)), but the address isn't confirmed, so **eat before you fly**.
- **Trastevere:** Donkey Punch sandwiches **to 04:00**; Cabullo, Osteria Cacio e Pepe, Wiki Wiki to 02:00 ([Pine Voyager](https://thepinevoyager.com/late-night-restaurants-in-rome/)).
- **Campo / Pantheon:** Romoletto, Cybo, Antica Salumeria (~€20) to 02:00. **Testaccio** is thin after 01:00.

### Tourist traps and bill scams

- **Red flags:** touts outside, photo menus, "menu turistico", tables facing Trevi, the Pantheon or Navona. Walking **8–12 minutes** away cuts prices ([Hungarian Abroad](https://hungarianabroad.com/rome-scams-and-tourist-traps-dont-get-played-in-2026/)): dinner for two is **€100–150 on Piazza Navona** vs **€60–80 in Trastevere or Testaccio** ([Rome Tourist](https://www.rometourist.net/rome-2026-price-guide-living-the-dolce-vita-without-losing-your-shirt-%F0%9F%87%AE%F0%9F%87%B9)).
- **Read the bill:** *coperto* (cover charge, legal and normal), *servizio* (service), bread and water can appear unasked. Say **"acqua del rubinetto"** for tap water. Fish "per etto" = per 100 g: ask the total first.
- **Ask for "il conto dettagliato"** (itemised bill). Tourists have been charged €430 for two plates of spaghetti; you may dispute, and if blocked from leaving, call **112** ([Wanted in Rome](https://www.wantedinrome.com/news/rip-off-rome-what-to-do-if-your-restaurant-bill-is-suspicious.html)).
- **Gelato:** avoid tall neon mountains; real pistachio is olive-brown ([Rome for Americans](https://www.romeforamericans.com/article/best-gelato-rome)).

**Aperitivo 19:00–21:00**, dinner until ~23:00, bars busiest **00:00–02:00**, clubs busiest **01:30–03:30** ([Europe Nightlife](https://europenightlife.com/italy/rome/rome-nightlife)). - **First date:** aperitivo 19:00–21:00 at a busy bar **you** choose (Freni e Frizioni, a Monti wine bar).
- **Italian norms:** people often meet through friends and at aperitivo; the man traditionally makes the first move, offers to pay and holds the door; be on time; greet with two cheek kisses; skip politics and religion; things move slowly ([Expatica](https://www.expatica.com/it/living/love/italy-dating-79378/)). Travellers at your hostel are often the most open to meeting up.

### Respect and consent

- Meet people in social settings; keep it conversational. **Take the first "no" gracefully.** No touching, following, catcalling, or repeatedly asking for a number or Instagram.
- **Consent must be clear, enthusiastic and ongoing**; someone very drunk can't consent. Tinder: "All sexual activity must start with consent and should include ongoing check-ins" ([Tinder](https://policies.tinder.com/safety/intl/en/)). Italy's parliament is still rewriting its consent law ([HRW](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/01/27/the-absence-of-no-is-not-yes-italys-flawed-sexual-violence-bill)); the safe standard is a clear yes.
- **App red flags:** moves you to WhatsApp immediately, asks for money, gift cards or crypto, or insists on one bar and orders pricey drinks. **Never send money.**

## 11. Getting around Rome

- **Walk first.** Pantheon → Colosseum is ~1.6 km (26 min); Navona → St Peter's ~1.4 km (23 min). In the centre, buses are often slower than walking.
- **Metro:** 05:30–23:30 Sun–Thu; **last trains ~01:30 on Fri and Sat** ([Roma Mobilità](https://romamobilita.it/muoversi-a-roma/linee-e-orari-di-metro-e-ferrovie/)). Some sites give other times, so check Citymapper.
- **Tap&Go:** tap your contactless card or phone at the gate or bus reader and wait for green. **Tap again on every new vehicle.** €1.50 per tap = 100 min + one metro ride. After 5 rides in 24 h the charge stops at **€8.50** (a day pass), **only if you always tap the same card or the same phone** (a card and its phone-wallet copy count as different) ([ATAC](https://www.atac.roma.it/biglietti-e-abbonamenti/tap-and-go); [ATAC FAQ](https://www.atac.roma.it/frequently-asked-questions/quanto-costa-il-biglietto-abbonamento-con-tap-go)). One card per person.
- **Paper tickets** (tabacchi, newsstands, machines): BIT **€1.50**, 24h **€8.50**, 48h **€15**, 72h **€22**, 7-day **€29**; validate every trip ([ATAC](https://www.atac.roma.it/biglietti-e-abbonamenti)). None covers the Leonardo Express. At 2–4 rides a day, Tap&Go (~€3–6) beats passes.

## 12. Safety, scams, rules and fines

**Big picture:** violent crime is low; pickpocketing is high in the centre and at big sights ([UK FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/italy/safety-and-security)). Passport in a zipped inner pocket, phone in a front pocket, backpack on your chest in crowds.

**Pickpocket hotspots:** **Metro A**, **buses 40 and 64**, **Termini platforms**, Trevi, the Colosseum queue, the Vatican entry lanes ([Roam Rome](https://www.roamrome.net/insights/is-rome-safe); [rome.info](https://www.rome.info/plan/scams/)).

| Scam | How it works | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Clip-joint bar** | A friendly stranger, often an attractive woman, invites you for a drink; the bill is hundreds of euros and bouncers block the door ([Rick Steves](https://www.ricksteves.com/travel-tips/theft-scams/tourist-scams)) | Only bars **you** choose; see a priced menu first |
| **Club touts** | "Free entry", then crazy prices and scary bouncers ([Following the Rivera](https://www.followingtherivera.com/scams-to-avoid-in-italy/)) | Refuse street offers |
| **Fake police** | Fake "Tourist Police" or "Guardia di Finanza" ask for your wallet | **Never hand it over**; ask for a badge number, offer a real station, call 112 ([World Nomads](https://www.worldnomads.com/travel-safety/europe/italy/common-scams-in-italy)) |
| **Bracelets / roses / gladiators** | "Gift" or photo, then a demand for money (gladiators "€5 and up") | "No, grazie", keep walking |
| **Petitions / machine "helpers"** | Distraction for a pickpocket, or a fee for "help" | Walk away; use Tap&Go or staff |
| **Padded bills** | Surprise charges (€81 for drinks near a landmark) | Priced menu, itemised bill, 112 if blocked |
| **Unlicensed taxis** | 2–3× the fare ([Roam Rome](https://www.roamrome.net/insights/is-rome-safe)) | Official rank or app only |
| **Drink spiking** | Victims "robbed and sometimes assaulted" ([FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/italy/safety-and-security)) | Never leave your drink; take drinks only from the bartender |
| **Fake dating profiles** | Fast move to WhatsApp, money requests, one "special" bar | Unmatch; never send money |

**Red zones (zone rosse):** police can remove people behaving aggressively. The **Esquilino** zone (January 2025 list) includes **Via Giolitti, Via Principe Amedeo, Via Gioberti, Via Cattaneo, Via Turati and Piazza Vittorio** ([Fanpage](https://www.fanpage.it/roma/zone-rosse-a-roma-firmata-lordinanza-lelenco-delle-strade-interdette-decise-dal-prefetto/)); since **24 Aug 2026** also **Colosseo, Fori Imperiali and Colle Oppio** (to 24 Dec), with San Lorenzo and Esquilino extended ([Il Post](https://www.ilpost.it/2026/08/28/roma-colosseo-zona-rossa-sicurezza/)). **At night** don't linger around Termini, Piazza dei Cinquecento or Esquilino side streets; don't cross **Colle Oppio park**; in San Lorenzo stay on busy streets.

**Laws that matter:**
- **Passport on you** at all times (section 3).
- **Drugs: don't.** Personal cannabis is an administrative offence, dealing is a crime, "cannabis light" flowers are banned ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_in_Italy)); the FCDO warns of "severe penalties". Street deals mean robbery risk, and a record could hurt future visas.
- **Night alcohol ban:** shops without seating can't sell takeaway alcohol **22:00–05:00 on Fri, Sat and Sun nights** across most of Rome, including the centre, Monti and Trastevere, **until 11 Oct 2026** (renewal after that unconfirmed). Bars keep serving ([CanaleDieci](https://canaledieci.it/2026/01/24/nuova-stretta-sulla-malamovida-alcol-vietato-di-notte-in-decine-di-quartieri-di-roma-la-mappa-dellordinanza/)).
- **No drinking from glass** on streets, transport or open parks after 22:00 ([Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/what-not-to-do-as-a-tourist-in-italy)).
- **No smoking** in bars, restaurants, clubs or public transport. Buying fake designer goods is fined too.

**If your passport is lost or stolen:**
1. In danger → **112**. Block your cards.
2. File a report at a **Polizia di Stato** or **Carabinieri** station: *denuncia di smarrimento* (loss) or *di furto* (theft). Bring passport photos/copies; **get a stamped copy**.
3. Call the **Turkish Embassy** (+39 06 445 941, or 24/7 +90 312 292 29 29) and ask for an urgent consular appointment.
4. Apply for an **emergency travel document** ("Pasaport Yerine Geçen Belge"); usually needs the police report, a photo and ID details (fee and timing not verified: ask the embassy).
5. **A police report alone won't get you out of Italy** ([FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/italy/safety-and-security)). Your visa was in the passport, so expect extra airport checks: go early.
6. Tell **Pegasus**.
