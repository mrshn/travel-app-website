# Travels together: friends, group trips, meeting people, and when plans change

Design and build spec, written 14 Oct 2026, two days after the Rome trip (Thu 8 to Mon 12 Oct 2026). Branch `together`.
It starts from what the Rome trip needed (section 1), records the decisions (section 3) and gives each package of the
build its contract (sections 6 to 10). Where a section names a file, that file is the contract; this document says why.

Everything here works on a 390 px phone held in one hand, at 320 px with no sideways scroll, in light and dark mode,
with 44 px touch targets, 4.5:1 text contrast, and no em dash (U+2014) in any new or changed file. The trip companion
keeps working offline; the social layer needs a connection and says so when there is none.

---

## 1. What the Rome trip needed

The owner's request (13 Oct 2026), in Turkish, verbatim:

> Roma planımızı genel sohbetten neler yaptık değerlendir. Nelere ihtiyacımız oldu neler konuştuk değerlendir. Bu
> ihtiyaçları karşılayabilcek daha iyi bir kullanıcı deneyimi sunan bir uygulama haline getir. Sosyalleşebileceğiz
> daha iyi yollar da ekleyelim uygulamaya. Plan paylaşabileceğimiz arkadaşlarımızla ortak plan yapabileceğimiz bir
> sosyal medya tarafı da ekleyelim uygulamaya.

In short: review what we did and needed for Rome; turn it into an app with a better experience for those needs; add
better ways to socialise; add a social side where we share plans and plan together with friends.

What happened, from the project notes (the plan of 28 Sep and 3 Oct, the planning chat, the claim of 9 Oct):

| Moment | What was done | What the app lacked |
|---|---|---|
| Planning, 28 Sep to 3 Oct | Questions first, deep research, a day-by-day plan with bookings and due dates, the hostel booked, a budget | Nothing: this became the app |
| Social goals | "Travel friends, dating, clubs and parties, chill bars and aperitivo". The plan's social toolkit: hostel events, Hostelworld City Chat and Linkups ("Anyone for the Vatican at 8 on Friday?"), free walking tours, pub crawls, a food tour, Tablo, language exchanges, dating apps, "swap Instagrams with your new hostel friends at breakfast" | All of it lived in other apps or in guide text. No way to post an open invite, keep the people you met, or tell anyone you got home |
| Sharing | The plan, the chat and an archived page were published in the public repository; the owner later decided to take the personal parts out (done in this build) | A private way to show a plan to friends, and to plan a trip with them |
| Thu 8 Oct, 22:08 | Wizz Air W4 6020 Barcelona to Rome was cancelled 23 minutes after its departure time. Rebooking was offered on Wizz only (Sunday); "refund" was picked and paid as Wizz credit; a Vueling ticket for Fri 10:55 cost €254.99; the hostel's first night (non-refundable) was lost; Friday morning, with the 08:00 Vatican slot, was gone | A way to re-plan in minutes (start later, move must-dos to another day), a checklist of what to do and not do at the desk, and somewhere to keep receipts |
| 9 Oct onwards | A claim was prepared outside the app: €579.39 plus meals and transport, with a deadline of 8 Dec 2026 and an escalation path (AESA) | A claims tracker with the deadline on the home screen, its amounts, steps and linked costs |

What worked and stays as it is: the live guide on Now, bookings with due dates, the cost keypad, places and stamps,
the offline map, accounts and sync, notes from chats.

## 2. Goals and non-goals

Goals, in the owner's words:

1. **"Ortak plan"**: plan a trip with friends. Invite them by link or QR, edit one shared plan, vote on options, chat,
   see who's in for each stop and where everyone is.
2. **"Plan paylaşabileceğimiz"**: share a plan. Let people follow a trip live, or send a copy anyone can open and copy.
3. **"Sosyal medya tarafı"**: friends and a feed of trip moments, with likes and comments; see which friends travel
   where and when.
4. **"Sosyalleşebileceğimiz daha iyi yollar"**: meet people on a trip. Open invites (meetups) with a link that works in
   any chat, tonight's options, friends in the same city, the people you met.
5. **"Nelere ihtiyacımız oldu"**: what the trip itself showed was missing: re-planning when plans change, and claims.

Not goals: public profiles or posts, follower counts, an algorithmic feed, push notifications (they need a server
this app doesn't have), live location tracking, bill splitting, direct messages outside a group trip, Cloud Functions,
composite Firestore indexes (the deploy account can't create them), and any change to how a solo trip works offline.

## 3. Decisions

**T1. The social layer sits on the account.** Friends, the feed, group trips, meetups and plan links need Google
sign-in and a connection. Offline, social screens show what they last had and say "You're offline". A remembered
account whose session lapsed sees "Sign in again to use friends and group trips". The solo trip companion is unchanged.

**T2. Friends are mutual, never followers.** Added by your link or QR (in person, or sent in any chat), by @handle, or
by a request from a shared context (a group trip, a meetup). Why: travel friends are people you met; a link or QR is
the in-app "swap Instagrams"; mutual friends keep posts private without per-post settings.

**T3. The feed is friends only, newest first, made on purpose.** A post is a moment: text and one photo, tagged with a
trip and a place; or a stop, a day recap, a plan link or a meetup. Likes and comments. Nothing is posted by itself.

**T4. A trip becomes a group trip when you invite someone.** Roles: organiser (owner), can edit, can view. One shared
plan; each person's ticks, ratings, notes, photos, costs, stamps, packing ticks, claims and people stay their own.

**T5. Options: your pick is your vote.** In a group trip, picking an option (the "Pick your Friday night" stops) also
votes for it; everyone sees the votes. The group's pick is the option's default in the shared plan: an editor makes the
leading choice the group pick in one tap. A personal pick still overrides it on your phone, and says so.

**T6. Group chat per trip,** with stops, photos and one-off locations, and system lines when the plan changes
("Ali moved Pantheon to 17:30"). Who's in for each stop (In, Maybe, Not me). A status per member ("At Pantheon",
"Running late", "Home safe"), set by hand or from Now.

**T7. Never tracked.** "Share where I am" sends one pin to the group chat; nothing follows you. Statuses are typed or
tapped.

**T8. Meetups are links.** An open invite for a time and a place ("Anyone for the Vatican at 8 on Friday?") at
/m/<id>, readable by anyone with the link (paste it into the hostel's WhatsApp or Hostelworld chat); saying "I'm in"
needs an account; who's going shows to signed-in people only. Made from a stop, a tonight idea, or from scratch.

**T9. Plan links are copies.** /p/<id> shows a read-only copy of a plan to anyone with the link, nothing personal in it
(where you stay only if you tick it). Signed in, they can copy it to their own trips on their own dates. You can
remove the link. Following a trip live is a group trip with "Can view".

**T10. Friends see your trips only if you say so.** "Let friends see where I'm going" (off until you turn it on)
shows friends the destination and dates of your trips. The app then shows "Friends in Rome on your dates" and friends
travelling now and soon.

**T11. Plans changed is one sheet with four situations:** running late (shift the rest of today), starting later
(skip what's gone, move the must-dos to a day they're open), a cancelled or delayed flight (what to ask for, what not
to accept in a hurry, receipts, tell your stay, start a claim), and taking the rest of the day off. Edits go through
the normal plan edits with Undo; booked stops never move; a group trip says what changed in its chat.

**T12. Claims live in the trip's progress** (private, synced): kind, company, references, incident date, deadline,
status, amounts with how likely each is, steps with dates, notes and links, and costs linked from the Costs page.
Deadlines show on Home from 60 days out. Templates for a cancelled or delayed flight (EU261 amounts by distance, worded
as a guide and not legal advice), a lost bag, a booking refund and anything else.

**T13. People you met live in the trip's progress** (private, synced): name, handle, where, a note, and "Send my
friend link".

**T14. Navigation stays five tabs.** The trip header gets a Together button (members' faces, or a person-plus icon on a
solo trip, with a dot for unread messages) that opens /trips/<id>/group. More gains a Together group (Group chat or Plan
with friends, Share this plan, Meet people) and an "If plans change" group (Plans changed, Claims). Home gains a
Friends button with a dot (requests, unread activity), a Friends section and a deadlines card. /friends holds the feed,
your friends and you.

**T15. A group trip is one Firestore document** (trips/{gid}) holding the plan as JSON, written only if the cloud still
has the version the write was based on, merged stop by stop (mergeTrips) when two people changed it. A group trip
leaves your personal items; a pointer in your account (users/{uid}/groups/{gid}) lets your other devices find it.

**T16. No composite indexes, no Cloud Functions, no push.** Every query is a single field or a document read. The feed
is written to each friend's feed (fan-out on write, at most 20 writes per batch for the rules' get() limit). Activity is
an in-app inbox. One new dependency: `uqr` (MIT, no dependencies) for QR codes.

**T17. Rules deny by default** and are tested on the emulators in CI (tests/e2e/social-cloud.e2e.mjs). Invite links are
capabilities: anyone with one can use it until its maker resets it.

**T18. A memory backend stands in for Firestore in browser checks** (NUXT_PUBLIC_SOCIAL_BACKEND=memory), with test hooks
to act as other people. Production always uses Firestore.

**T19. English, in the app's voice**: plain, warm, British spelling, sentence case, no exclamation marks in buttons.

**T20. The Rome sample is generic** (done before this build: id `rome-sample`, no hostel or flight details) and the
personal research and chat notes moved to the private Travel project. The owner's own trip (`rome-2026-10`) is
untouched: it simply no longer follows a seed.

**T21. Ids.** Group trips, invites, meetups and plan links use socialToken() (22 characters). A joiner's copy of a group
trip has the group id as its local id; the organiser's keeps its own id (its progress and photos stay linked).

**T22. A group trip's copy follows its role.** Viewers can't change the plan (the UI hides editing and the rules
refuse); a removed member, or everyone when the organiser deletes the group, keeps a personal copy ("No longer shared:
your copy stays on this device").

## 5. Screens

Copy in quotes is the copy to use. Numbers are examples.

### 5.1 Together (`/trips/<id>/group`), P2

Solo trip:

```
TOGETHER
Plan this trip with friends
Invite people to see the plan, change it with you, vote on options and chat.
Your ticks, costs, notes and photos stay yours.
[ Invite people ]                     (opens ?invite=1)
[ Let people follow along ]           (?invite=1&role=viewer)
[ Share a copy of the plan ]          (?share=1)
```

Group trip: header "Together", the members' faces, [Invite]. A segment: Chat · People · Decide (remembered per device).

- **Chat**: newest at the bottom, the composer fixed above the tab bar (text, then a + menu: Photo, A stop, Where I
  am). Bubbles: yours on the right in --accent-soft, others' on the left with face and first name; system lines centred
  and small; a stop as a card (title, day and time; tap opens the stop); a location as a card with "Open in Maps"; a
  photo full width (tap to view). A day divider ("Fri 9 Oct"). Empty: "Say hello. Everyone in this trip can read the
  chat." Offline: the composer says "You're offline. Messages send when you're back." and is disabled.
- **People**: each member with face, name, role, and status ("At Pantheon · 5 min ago", "Home safe · 01:45"). Yours on
  top with quick statuses: On my way, Running late, At <the stop on now>, Free now, Home safe, and Other (a line of
  text); "Share where I am" (one fix, sent to the chat). The organiser can change roles and remove people. "Leave this
  trip" (not the organiser), or "Stop sharing this trip" (organiser: deletes the group; everyone keeps a copy).
- **Decide**: option stops with their votes ("Pick your Friday night: Pub crawl 3, Hostel party 1"), the group pick
  marked, and for editors "Make it the group pick" on the leading choice; then the next stops with who's in.

### 5.2 Invite people (`?invite=1`), P2

"Invite people" · segment [Can edit | Can view] · the link (read-only field) with [Copy] [Share…] · [Show QR] (a QR of
the link, 200 px, on white) · "Send to friends": friends with [Invite] (an inbox item with the link) · "Links": the
working links with their role and [Reset] · a line: "Anyone with the link can join as Can edit until you reset it."
On a solo trip the first link makes it a group trip ("This makes Rome a group trip: people you invite see the plan.
Your ticks, costs, notes and photos stay yours.").

### 5.3 Join a trip (`/j/<token>`), P2

The trip's picture (tripCover), title, dates; "Emre invited you to plan this trip together" or "…to follow this trip";
what that means ("You'll see the plan, chat and vote. Your own ticks, costs and notes stay yours."). Signed out:
[Sign in with Google to join]. Signed in: [Join Rome]; then "Joining…" until the plan has arrived, then the trip's Plan
(Now when it is on). Reset link: "This link was reset. Ask Emre for a new one." Already in: [Open Rome]. Your own:
"This is your invite link. Share it with the people you want to join."

### 5.4 Votes, who's in and status in the trip screens, P2

- **ChoicePicker** in a group trip: under each choice, the faces of who voted for it and a count; a gold "Group pick"
  chip on the option's default; picking a choice also votes; "You picked X. The group picked Y." with [Follow the group]
  when they differ; editors get [Make it the group pick] on the leading choice.
- **StopRow**: up to three faces of who's in, then "+2".
- **StopTogether** (stop sheet, group trips, not minor stops): "Who's in?" with [I'm in] [Maybe] [Not me] (pressed
  state), the faces of each, and [Send to chat].
- **NowGroupStrip** (group trips, during the trip): one line of faces with statuses ("Ali: At Pantheon · Can: Home
  safe"), the unread count, tap to the chat.
- **HomeCard** (late night): [I'm home safe] (group trips: sets the status and tells the chat; solo: "Let someone know"
  shares "Home safe, 01:45." through the phone's share sheet).
- **StopEditor**: viewers can't open it; an editor's change to a group trip says what changed in the chat.

### 5.5 Friends (`/friends`), P3

Header "Friends", a bell (Activity: ?inbox=1) with a dot, [Add friends]. Segment Feed · Friends · You (in the URL).

- **Feed**: a prompt card "Share a moment from your trip" (opens ?compose=1), then posts: face, name, time, trip and
  place chips, text, photo, the recap line, a link card (plan or meetup), [♡ 3] [Comment 2] (… for yours: Delete).
  "Show more". Empty: "No posts yet. When friends share moments from their trips, they show up here." [Add friends].
- **Friends**: "Requests" (Accept, Decline; yours: Waiting, Cancel); "Travelling now" and "Soon" (from friends' cards);
  all friends (face, name, @handle, "In Rome until 12 Oct") opening ?friend=<uid>; "Add friends": [Share my link]
  [Show my QR] and "Find by @handle".
- **You**: your card (photo, name, @handle, bio, home city; edit in place), "Let friends see where I'm going" (switch),
  "Your friend link" (QR, Copy, Share, Reset), "Your plan links" (Copy, Remove), "Your meetups" (opens /m/<id>).

### 5.6 Posts, P3

- **Composer** (?compose=1): face and "Posting to your friends"; text (2,000); one photo (from the phone, or one of the
  stop's photos); chips for the trip and place (removable); [Post]. Presets (usePostComposer): from a stop (its title
  and place, its photos to pick), a day (the recap line "11 stops · 4 stamps · ★★★★"), a plan link, a meetup.
- **Post sheet** (?post=<id>): the post, its likes ("Liked by Ali and 2 others"), comments (oldest first) and a field
  to comment. Delete your comment, or any comment on your post.
- **Activity** (?inbox=1): newest first; each line opens its link; unread marked; "Mark all read".
- **A friend** (?friend=<uid>): face, name, handle, bio, home city; their trips (if they share them), with "You're
  both in Rome on 10 to 12 Oct" when they meet yours; their recent posts (from your feed); [Unfriend].

### 5.7 Add a friend (`/f/<token>`), P3

Their face, name, handle, bio, home city. Signed out: [Sign in with Google to add Emre]. Signed in: [Add Emre as a
friend], then "You and Emre are friends" with [Open Friends]. Already friends; your own link; a reset link
("This link was reset. Ask for a new one.").

### 5.8 Home, P3 and P5

Header: logo; Notes; Theme; **Friends** (users icon, dot for requests or unread activity); Account; Settings. At 320 px
the header keeps 44 px buttons (the word "Travels" gives way first, as today).
`HomeDeadlines` (P5): "Wizz Air claim: send by 8 Dec · 55 days left" (warn tone from 14 days, bad when overdue), each
opening its claim. `HomeFriends` (P3): no friends yet: "Travel is better with friends. Add friends to share moments and
plan trips together." [Add friends]; else "Travelling now" faces with places, the two newest posts, [Open the feed].
TripCard: a group trip shows its members' faces.

### 5.9 Meet people (`/trips/<id>/meet`), P4

1. **Tonight** (during the trip): today's night card and the ideas for today; group trips: who's in for tonight's
   night stops; [Ask who's in] (a meetup for tonight).
2. **Your meetups** for this trip, with who's going.
3. **Friends here**: friends in the same place on these dates (from their cards) and the trip's people; [Say hi] (a
   meetup invite or a chat).
4. **Ways to meet people**: Trip.meet ideas (the Rome sample gets them), or general ones for any city (hostel events,
   a free walking tour, a pub crawl, a language exchange, aperitivo, a food tour, meetup apps), each with its link and
   [Ask who's in] (a meetup prefilled from its `ask`).
5. **People you met**: name, handle, where, when; [Add someone] (?met=new); each opens ?met=<id> with "Send my friend
   link" (shares /f/<your token>) and Delete.

### 5.10 Meetups, P4

- **Sheet** (?meetup=new from a stop, an idea, or blank): title, a note (prefilled "Anyone for the Vatican at 8 on
  Friday?"), day and time (from the stop), place (from the stop, or none); [Create the link]; then the link with
  [Copy] [Share…] [Post to friends] [Send to the group chat] (group trips). ?meetup=<id>: edit, cancel ("Cancel this
  meetup"), who's going.
- **Page** (/m/<id>): the host's face and name; title; when ("Fri 9 Oct, 08:00 Rome time", and "09:00 your time"
  when it differs); where, with [Directions]; the note; who's going (signed in only; signed out: "Sign in to see who's
  going"); [I'm in] / [I can't make it] (signed out: [Sign in with Google to join]); [Add to calendar] (an .ics); for
  the host: [Edit] [Share again] [Cancel meetup]; passed: "This meetup has passed."; cancelled: "Emre cancelled this
  meetup."

### 5.11 Share this plan (`?share=1`) and plan links (`/p/<id>`), P4

Sheet: four rows: **Plan it together** (?invite=1) · **Let people follow along** (?invite=1&role=viewer) ·
**Share a copy of the plan** (makes a plan link: "Include where I stay" off by default; then Copy, Share, Post to
friends) · **Post to your friends** (the composer with the plan link). Page /p/<id>: the cover, title, dates, "Shared by
Emre", each day's stops (time, title, kind icon; options as their default choice), bookings and packing counts;
[Copy to my trips] with a start date (default: the same dates if they're ahead, else the next date with the same
weekday); signed out: [Sign in with Google to copy this plan]; removed: "This plan link was removed."

### 5.12 Plans changed (`?changed=1`), P5

A sheet with four cards; each opens its own step in the same sheet, with Back.

1. **Running late**: "How late?" [15 min] [30] [45] [1 h] [1 h 30]; the stops that move (old → new time), booked stops
   marked "Stays: booked" and any clash warned; [Move 6 stops]. Undo in the toast.
2. **Starting later**: "I'll start at [time]" (default: the next quarter hour); stops before then that will be
   skipped; must-dos and booked ones each with "Move to Sat 10" (a day the place is open, picked by
   shared/utils/changes.ts) or "Skip"; [Apply].
3. **Flight cancelled or delayed**: a checklist to tick ("Ask for rerouting on another airline before accepting a
   refund or credit", "Ask for meal vouchers and a hotel in writing", "Keep every receipt and screenshot",
   "Screenshot the cancellation email and the app", "Ask for the reason in writing", "Tell your stay you'll arrive
   later", "Check which bookings you can move"), a message to the place you stay (English and the local language) to
   copy, [Start a claim] (a claim from the template), [Re-plan tomorrow] (step 2 for the next day). "This is a guide,
   not legal advice."
4. **Taking the rest of today off**: skips what's left today, Undo.

Entry points: Now's `PlansChangedLink` ("Plans changed? Running late, starting later or a cancelled flight"), Plan's
`PlanDayTools` ("Plans changed?" for that day), More.

### 5.13 Claims (`/trips/<id>/claims`, `?claim=…`), P5

The page lists claims: title, company, status chip, deadline line, expected total ("€579.39 · likely €255"), steps
done ("2 of 6"). [New claim] → a template (Flight cancelled, Flight delayed, Lost or late bag, Booking refund, Other).
The sheet: title and status; company, booking reference, their claim reference; incident date, deadline (prefilled
incident + 2 months for flights, with "Check their own deadline"), sent on; amounts (label, amount, how likely, paid);
linked costs (from the Costs ledger: toggle "Part of this claim"); steps (tick, due date, add); note; links. Delete.
Costs: the cost sheet's edit mode gets "Part of a claim" (P5's small change to CostSheet.vue).

## 6. Data and the cloud

Types: `shared/types/social.ts` (new) and `shared/types/trip.ts` (Trip.group, Trip.tombstones, Trip.copiedFrom,
Trip.meet, Stop.updatedAt, Expense.claimId, TripProgress.claims and .people, Claim, MetPerson, MeetIdea).

### 6.1 Firestore layout

| Path | Holds | Read | Write |
|---|---|---|---|
| `users/{uid}/items/{item}` | trips and progress (as before) | you | you (as before) |
| `users/{uid}/groups/{gid}` | GroupPointer | you | you |
| `users/{uid}/feed/{postId}` | FeedItem | you; its author | create: its author, if you're friends (or it's yours); delete: you or its author |
| `users/{uid}/inbox/{id}` | InboxItem | you | create: from = the writer, and (friends, or kind friend-request, or kind meetup-going to the meetup's host, or trip-invite / joined-trip between people of one group trip); update (read): you; delete: you |
| `profiles/{uid}` | Profile | signed in | you (name 1 to 60, photo URL or null, bio 160, homeCity 60, handle only as handles/ says) |
| `handles/{handle}` | `{ uid }` | signed in | create: your uid, a valid handle; delete: yours |
| `cards/{uid}` | FriendCard | you or a friend | you |
| `invites/{token}` | Invite | get: anyone; list: your own (from == you) | create: from = you (trip invites: owner or editor of that trip, role editor or viewer); update: only `revoked: true`, by its maker; no delete |
| `friendships/{a_b}` | Friendship | its members | create: you're a member, id = sorted pair, and via is a working friend invite of the other, or 'request' with requests/{other}_{you} present; delete: a member |
| `requests/{from}_{to}` | FriendRequest | from or to | create: from = you, to ≠ you; delete: from or to |
| `posts/{postId}` | Post | in its audience | create: author = you, you in the audience (at most 500), text 2,000; update: the author (text, audience, photo) or, for anyone in the audience, only their own key of `likes`; delete: the author |
| `posts/{postId}/comments/{id}` | Comment | the post's audience | create: in the audience, author = you, text 1 to 1,000; delete: its author or the post's author |
| `trips/{gid}` | GroupTripDoc | its members | create: owner = you, roles = {you: owner}; update: owner and editors change only json, updatedAt (going up), title, destination, start, end, cover; anyone joins with a working invite (only their own roles and people keys, and joinedVia); a member leaves (removes only their own keys; not the owner) or updates their own people entry; the owner changes others' roles; delete: the owner |
| `trips/{gid}/messages/{id}` | Message | members | create: a member, author = you, text 1,000; delete: its author |
| `trips/{gid}/votes/{stopId}__{uid}` | Vote | members | you, for your own id |
| `trips/{gid}/rsvps/{stopId}__{uid}` | Rsvp | members | you, for your own id |
| `trips/{gid}/status/{uid}` | MemberStatus | members | you |
| `meetups/{id}` | Meetup | get: anyone; list: the host's own | create and update: the host |
| `meetups/{id}/going/{uid}` | MeetupGoing | signed in | you, your own |
| `shares/{id}` | Share | get: anyone; list: the owner's own | create, update (revoked, and json emptied), delete: the owner |

Storage: `users/{uid}/public/{id}.jpg` (posts and chat photos): read signed in (the app shows them by their download
URL); write you, images under 4 MB. `users/{uid}/photos/` stays as it is.

Limits kept in mind: rules allow 10 get()/exists() per document write and 20 per batch, so fan-out batches hold at most
10 writes. Queries are single-field (equality, array-contains, or one orderBy), so no composite index is needed.

### 6.2 What the backend does (app/lib/social/api.ts is the contract)

- **Posting**: write posts/{id} with the audience, then a FeedItem into users/{f}/feed/{id} for you and each friend,
  in batches of 10. Deleting removes the copies by the audience.
- **New friends**: whoever sees a new friendship (both sides' live list) shares their recent posts (20) with the
  friend: adds them to those posts' audience and writes the feed copies. Remembered per device and per pair.
- **Unfriend**: deletes the friendship; removes your posts from their feed and them from your posts' audiences;
  removes their posts from your feed.
- **Likes**: a map in the post; counts read from the post. **Comments**: a subcollection; the count with
  getCountFromServer.
- **Group trips**: see section 7.

### 6.3 What each device keeps

`localStorage`: `travel:social:v1:<uid>` (profiles seen, friends, the last feed page, the last 100 messages per group,
read marks, the friend link token): for offline reading. `travel:groups:sync:v1` (per account: what each group trip
last agreed with the cloud). Both are removed with the device's copy (useCloud.clearLocal). The memory backend's
"cloud" (`travel:fakecloud:v1`) is not.

## 7. Group trips: the shared plan

- **Making one** (useGroups.makeGroup, on the first invite): a group id; trips/{gid} with the plan
  (socialGroupJson: id = gid, no group, seed or edited fields), you as owner; your pointer {gid, localId: the trip's
  id, role: owner}; your copy gets `group: { id, role }` and loses `seedId`, `seedVersion`. Its personal item
  (users/{uid}/items/trip-<id>) is deleted (a tombstone) by useCloud, which leaves group trips out of the items.
- **Joining** (useGroups.join): joinGroup(token) adds you; your pointer {gid, localId: gid}; the plan arrives and
  becomes your copy (socialGroupTrip).
- **Keeping in step** (useGroups' engine, one per account, for every pointer): watch trips/{gid}; remember per gid the
  hash of the JSON and the updatedAt last agreed. On a snapshot or a local change (800 ms after it):
  - no copy here: make it (a personal trip with the same id, as on the organiser's second device, is adopted: merged);
  - only the cloud moved: take it (keeping your id and role);
  - only this copy moved: write it if you can edit (else put the cloud's back);
  - both moved: mergeTrips(yours, theirs), keep it, write it.
  Writes are conditional on the updatedAt they were based on (writeGroupJson returns false when someone else wrote
  first; the next snapshot decides again). Versions only go up.
- **Edits that merge well**: useTrips stamps `updatedAt` on every stop it adds, changes or moves, and leaves a tombstone
  for every stop, booking or packing item it removes (keys in shared/utils/tripmerge.ts). Group picks change the
  option's `default` (useTrips.updateStop).
- **Gone**: when trips/{gid} can't be read any more (deleted, or you were removed), your copy loses its group link and
  stays as a personal trip; the pointer is deleted; a toast says so once.
- **Leaving**: leaveGroup, delete the pointer, keep the copy as a personal trip (or remove it, if you choose).
- **Personal things stay personal**: progress (ticks, costs, stamps, claims, people) syncs through your items as
  before, keyed by your local id.

## 12. As built: decisions taken while building (for the owner to confirm)

Each package recorded what the spec left open; the lead took the rest while putting them together.

**The cloud (P1).** The rules are stricter than 6.1 in three places: profiles and handles can be read one at a time but
not listed; invite, meetup and plan-link ids must be 16 to 64 characters (so nobody can claim a guessable id); handles
follow socialHandleValid. The backend checks lengths on the device first, so an over-long text is 'invalid' before it is
'denied'. Changing the role of someone not in the trip is 'not-found'; joining a trip you're already in returns it even if
the link was reset since. The organiser's "Stop sharing" deletes the chat, votes, answers and statuses first. Every
emulator check (22 steps, over 90 rules cases) passed on the first run in CI.

**Group trips (P2).** Together's tab (Chat, People, Decide) is in the URL and remembered per device. Chat photos are
shrunk to 1280 px. "Share where I am" takes one fix and stops the location at once. The stop editor refuses to open for a
viewer with a toast; Add to plan in a place's sheet is hidden for viewers. An editor's change says in the chat what
changed: added, moved, changed or removed, with the stop's name.

**Friends and the feed (P3).** The Friends button sits between Theme and Account on Home; with five buttons the word
"Travels" hides below 425 px (the logo stays). A trip invite opened at /f/ goes to /j/. The landing page has an eighth
feature card, Together.

**Meet and share (P4).** "Say hi" to a friend in town opens a meetup prefilled for them (`?meetup=new&hi=<uid>`);
editing a meetup from its page opens the trip's sheet on the form (`&change=1`). A plan link's page lists each day's main
stops and "+ N small steps". General ideas (for trips without `meet`) use ids starting `any-` and search links for the
destination. The Rome sample has 12 ways to meet people.

**Plans changed and claims (P5).** Running late also moves the stop that is on now if it isn't ticked; stops that ended
unticked never move. Taking the rest of the day off leaves booked stops, flights and trains in the plan, listed as "Still
in your plan". Small logistics steps never count as must-dos; a moved stop keeps its time on its new day. A new claim is
made at once (`?claim=new`) with Undo in the toast; its date is the trip's first day (its last day once the trip has
ended); the bag template's deadline is 7 days; the EU261 line starts as "Unsure" (it depends on the airline's reason);
templates carry no web links (none could be checked from the build machine).

**Integration (lead).** The sheet opened last is on top, whichever was mounted first (a post composer over a stop sheet,
a cost sheet over a claim sheet). The × in a sheet's drag strip keeps its click with a mouse. A friend's card is read
again after ten minutes. The social layer reads 'loading', not 'signed out', while a remembered session is being picked
up. A group trip's live lists (votes, who's in, statuses) survive a new backend (signing in again, back online).
